### Six core features (verbatim blurbs — useful for /features Phase 2)
1. **Cloud Services** — Monitor and manage unlimited sales, employees, inventory, reports and multiple stores.
2. **Work Offline** — Keep recording sales and run operation even when offline and sync database to cloud when online.
3. **Inventory Management** — Create purchase orders, goods receive notes, estimate, inventory history report and manage stock.
4. **Employee Management** — Create users, commission staff, manage access right, login alerts and sales by employee.
5. **Financial Management** — Automation sales summary with an unrestricted date filter to accurately calculate net profit before accounting for the cost of goods sold, operational expenditures, and overhead.
6. **Marketing Management** — Collect database information to identify the most popular and best-selling items, and analyze customer behavior to enhance upselling strategies and utilize redemption points to foster loyalty.
